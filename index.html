I believe a good frame should make you feel something.

FILMVYN is a creative visual portfolio built around video editing, color grading, visual composition, framing, and scriptwriting.

My approach is simple: understand the story first, then use the edit, color, sound, and composition to make that story feel intentional.

From cinematic edits to short-form content, I focus on creating visuals that feel natural, atmospheric, and memorable.
